Our Story

Born from a love of nature and a commitment to ethical, sustainable dining.

Our Core Philosophy

Plant-First

Every dish is built around plants, not built around removing meat. We celebrate vegetables as the hero ingredient.

Community-Rooted

We employ locally, source locally and give back locally. 15% of profits fund community food education.

Zero Waste

Our kitchen composts 98% of food waste. All packaging is compostable. We measure and report quarterly.

Carbon-Neutral

We've offset our full carbon footprint since 2022, certified by the Carbon Trust.

How LeafTable Came to Be

2018

A Dream in a Small Kitchen

Founded by Chef Marco Viani and sustainability advocate Lena Brooks in a rented Hackney kitchen. What started as a supper club for 12 friends became an instant sensation. Within weeks, the waiting list had grown to 300 people.

2019

Opening Our Doors

We opened our first 40-seat restaurant on Shoreditch's Bethnal Green Road, designed around reclaimed wood, living plant walls and natural light. Every piece of furniture was sourced from London ethical makers.

2021

Recognition & Growth

Awarded 'Best Vegan Restaurant London' by TimeOut Magazine and featured in the Guardian's Top 50 UK Restaurants. We expanded to a second dining room, adding a private chef's table seating 12.

2023

Zero Waste & Rooftop Garden

Launched our zero-waste initiative, achieving 98% waste diversion. Opened our 200sqm rooftop herb and microgreen garden, supplying 30% of our fresh herbs in-house. Partnered with 24 local organic farms.

2026

Eight Years of Joyful Dining

Celebrating eight years and 12,000+ happy guests. This year we launched our Vegan Cookery School, Farm Dinner Series and this new website to bring the LeafTable story to even more plant-based food lovers.

Growing Responsibly

Sustainability isn't a marketing term for us — it's the reason we exist.

Farm Partnerships

We partner with 24 certified organic farms within 50 miles of London. Every supplier is visited annually and held to our strict ethical sourcing charter.

24 Farm Partners • 50-mile radius

Zero Waste Kitchen

98% of our kitchen waste is composted or repurposed. Food off-cuts become staff meals. We produce less than 2kg of landfill waste per week — for a 60-seat restaurant.

98% Waste Diverted • <2kg to Landfill/Week

Water Stewardship

We use 60% less water than a conventional restaurant of our size, through grey-water recycling, low-flow equipment and rigorous team training.

60% Less Water • Fully Recycled Grey Water
