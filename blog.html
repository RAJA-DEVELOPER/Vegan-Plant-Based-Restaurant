Eating with the Seasons: A Guide to Autumn Plant-Based Cooking

Chef Marco Viani · Nov 15, 2026 · 8 min read

As the days shorten and the air carries that first bite of autumn chill, the kitchen transforms into something altogether more warming and deeply satisfying. Gone are the abundant summer tomatoes and courgettes; in their place arrive the magnificent root vegetables, brassicas, wild mushrooms and squashes that define the season. At LeafTable, we don't fight the seasons — we surrender to them completely. Our menu shifts with the harvest, following what our partner farms tell us is at its absolute peak. This autumn, we're celebrating the extraordinary versatility of heritage cauliflower, the deep earthiness of celeriac, the vibrant bitterness of purple sprouting broccoli, and the unmatched comfort of a perfectly made butternut squash risotto. In this guide, I want to share our kitchen philosophy for cooking with the seasons, along with three recipes you can recreate at home this weekend…
