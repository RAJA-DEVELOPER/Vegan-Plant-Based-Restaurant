Getting Here

By Tube

Shoreditch High Street Overground — 2 min walk east.

Liverpool Street (Central / Circle / Metropolitan) — 10 min walk north-west.

By Bus

Routes 8, 26 and 55 stop directly outside on Garden Lane.

Night buses N8, N26 run Fri–Sat until 4am for late dining.

By Bike

Santander Cycle docking station 50m from the restaurant on Garden Lane. Secure cycle parking for 20 bikes at our rear entrance — just ask staff.

By Car

NCP Car Park, Commercial Street — 10-min walk. £4/hr. We provide a 2-hour validation stamp with any dinner booking of 2+ courses. Congestion charge zone — check TfL.

Frequently Asked Questions

Absolutely! Children are welcome at all sittings. We have high chairs available and a dedicated children's plant-based menu (ask when booking). The Chef's Table is recommended for guests aged 12 and over due to the nature of the extended dining experience.

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in our outdoor terrace area (weather permitting, April–October). We provide a water bowl and a small plant-based dog treat on arrival. Indoor dining areas are for humans only, except certified assistance dogs.

We describe our dress code as 'smart casual with personality'. Jeans and trainers are absolutely fine — we just ask guests to feel comfortable and confident. The Chef's Table has a slightly elevated expectation of smart casual. No sportswear please.
