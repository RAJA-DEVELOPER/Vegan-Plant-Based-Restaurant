The Chef's Table

A 7-Course Vegan Journey

4.9 from 847 reviews

What to Expect

Step into something genuinely rare: an intimate, theatrical, and deeply immersive dining experience unlike anything you've encountered before. The Chef's Table is a 12-seat private dining room positioned at the heart of our open kitchen, where the sights, sounds and aromas of the kitchen become as much a part of the evening as the food itself. Every seat offers an unobstructed view of the pass, the flames, and the hands that craft each dish with quiet precision.

The evening unfolds as live cooking theatre. Head Chef Marco Viani moves through the kitchen with the ease of a seasoned performer, introducing each course as it takes shape before you — sharing the provenance of every ingredient, the farm or forager behind it, and the technique that transforms it. It's education, entertainment and artistry rolled into one seamless, unhurried experience.

Complementing each course is a carefully curated pairing experience from our resident sommelier: either a natural, low-intervention wine flight sourced from biodynamic producers across Europe, or our house-crafted botanical juice pairing — both entirely zero-sulphite. The juice pairings are as complex and considered as the wines, and many guests prefer them. Both are optional add-ons at +$35 per person.

The Chef's Table is not a rushed meal — it is an entire evening's entertainment. The experience runs approximately three hours, with unhurried pacing between courses designed to give you time to absorb, discuss, and savour every element. By the time the petit fours and herbal teas arrive to close the evening, you'll have shared a conversation with your chef, a story with your ingredients, and a memory with your table.

Tonight's Journey

Every course tells a story of the season, the soil, and the hands that grew it.

I

Amuse-Bouche

"Truffle Foam on Celeriac Crisp"

A single perfect bite: house-whipped truffle foam atop a wafer-thin celeriac crisp, finished with chive oil and a micro-herb garnish. An awakening for the palate before the journey truly begins.

Black Truffle
II

Cold Starter

"Heirloom Tomato & Basil Water Consommé"

A crystal-clear consommé of slow-pressed heirloom tomatoes, served chilled with a bruschetta of torn basil and sea salt. The simplicity of perfection — nothing hidden, everything celebrated.

Heirloom Tomatoes
III

Warm Starter

"Wild Mushroom Velouté"

A silky velouté of porcini, shiitake and oyster mushrooms, finished with a swirl of cashew cream, truffle oil and crispy shallots. Deep, earthy and profoundly comforting — a course guests speak of long after the meal.

Wild Porcini
IV

Palate Cleanser

"Cucumber, Mint & Elderflower Granita"

A refreshing interlude: hand-scraped granita of English cucumber and elderflower, with a mint oil finish and candied lemon zest. Light as first light, cold as a country morning — clearing the way for what's to come.

Elderflower
V

Main Course

"Pan-Roasted Heritage Cauliflower, Hazelnut Romesco"

A whole roasted cauliflower steak, blackened at its edges, served on a river of hazelnut romesco with pickled raisins, herb oil and crispy capers. The humble made extraordinary — the heart of the evening's story.

Heritage Cauliflower
VI

Cheese Course

"Artisan Cashew Cheeses & Walnut Crackers"

Three house-aged cashew cheeses: truffle-ash, herb-crusted and smoked paprika, served with walnut crackers, quince jelly and grapes. A revelation for cheese lovers and sceptics alike — rich, complex and deeply satisfying.

Aged Cashew
VII

Dessert

"Dark Chocolate & Raspberry Tart, Oat Cream"

A glossy 70% dark chocolate tart in a hazelnut pastry shell, topped with fresh raspberries, raspberry coulis and a quenelle of vanilla oat cream. A finale as memorable as the journey — bittersweet, vivid and complete.

70% Dark Chocolate

We Accommodate with Care

We can prepare the full menu entirely gluten-free with 48 hours advance notice — every course is carefully adapted without compromising the vision. Nut-free menus are available with 72 hours advance notice; some courses will be thoughtfully reimagined using alternative techniques and ingredients.

All dishes are 100% plant-based, dairy-free and egg-free as standard — this is a fully vegan kitchen, and not a single concession has been made in flavour or creativity for it.

Allergen Notice: Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, sesame and soy. Please inform us of all allergies and intolerances when booking so we can ensure a safe and spectacular evening for every guest.
Dress code: Smart casual. We kindly ask guests to refrain from strong fragrances.
Not suitable for children under 12 years of age.
