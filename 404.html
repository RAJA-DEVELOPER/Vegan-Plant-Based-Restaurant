5-Minute Avocado & Hemp Seed Toast

5 min Serves 1–2 Vegan & GF option High Protein

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough or GF bread
  • 1 large ripe avocado, halved & pitted
  • 2 tbsp hemp seeds
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Flaky sea salt & black pepper
  • Handful of microgreens or fresh basil
  • Optional: 1 tsp nutritional yeast

Method

  1. Toast your bread to golden perfection. While it's toasting, scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl and mash it gently with a fork — leaving some chunky texture for extra character.
  2. Season the mashed avocado with lemon juice, a pinch of sea salt, black pepper, and nutritional yeast if using. Stir to combine, then spread generously over your toast.
  3. Finish with a shower of hemp seeds and red pepper flakes. Pile on fresh microgreens or torn basil leaves. Serve immediately and enjoy every plant-powered bite!
LeafTable Tip: Add sliced cherry tomatoes or a drizzle of tahini for extra richness. Great post-workout or as a weekend brunch treat!
